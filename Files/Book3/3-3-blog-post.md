# Free Online Planning Clinics for New ClearPath Customers

*Published August 24, 2026*

Starting September 10, ClearPath will offer free online planning clinics for customers who are setting up their first project.

The clinics will take place every Thursday at 1:00 p.m. ET. Each session will last 30 minutes and will be hosted by a ClearPath project specialist. Customers can bring one question about choosing a goal, dividing work into smaller tasks, or setting a realistic completion date.

## What to expect

During each clinic, the specialist will demonstrate a simple first-project setup and answer submitted questions. The clinics are designed for new customers, so no previous project-planning experience is required.

Attendance is free, but registration is required because each clinic is limited to 20 customers. Registration closes at noon ET on the day before each session.

## How to register

To request a place, email [clinics@clearpath.example](mailto:clinics@clearpath.example) with your name and preferred clinic date. ClearPath will reply by email to confirm whether a place is available and provide the link for joining the online session.
