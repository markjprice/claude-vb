# ClearPath Workshop Program Guide

> Fictional sample file for *ChatGPT Visual Bible*. All people, organizations, services, and policies in this file are invented for teaching.

## Program purpose

ClearPath runs practical workshops that help adults plan and complete a first project. Workshops should give each participant a small result they can use before they leave.

## Intended audience

- Adults who are planning a first personal, community, study, or work project.
- People who may not know project-management terms.
- People who prefer short activities and examples over long lectures.

## Standard workshop

- Title: Plan Your First Project
- Length: 90 minutes
- Recommended attendance: 8 to 16 people
- Maximum attendance: 20 people
- Delivery: In person or live online
- Standard ticket price: £15 per person
- Each participant receives a one-page project plan and a digital checklist.

## Required agenda

1. Welcome and outcome, 5 minutes
2. Choose one clear goal, 15 minutes
3. Divide the goal into smaller tasks, 20 minutes
4. Break, 10 minutes
5. Choose an achievable completion date, 15 minutes
6. Identify one likely obstacle, 10 minutes
7. Complete and review the one-page plan, 10 minutes
8. Questions and next step, 5 minutes

## Room and equipment

- Step-free access or a clearly stated alternative arrangement
- Accessible restroom
- Seats and writing surfaces for every participant
- Projector or large display
- Reliable Wi-Fi for the facilitator
- Quiet area away from unrelated activities
- Drinking water

For an online workshop, use live captions and send the worksheet at least 24 hours before the session.

## Budget and approval rules

- The normal total budget is £450 per workshop.
- Venue hire above £180 needs written approval from the program manager.
- Paid advertising above £75 needs written approval from the program manager.
- Do not book a venue, buy advertising, or publish an event page without human approval.
- Record prices with the date and source because public prices can change.

## Cancellation and changes

- Review registrations 72 hours before the workshop.
- If fewer than six people have registered, ask the program manager whether to cancel, combine, or continue with the session.
- Tell registered participants before announcing a cancellation publicly.
- Do not promise a refund or replacement date unless a human has approved it.

## Success measures

- Attendance rate: attendees divided by registrations
- Average satisfaction score, excluding missing scores
- Percentage of participants who finish a one-page project plan
- Total delivery cost
- Cost per attendee

## Approved claims

You may say that the workshop is practical, beginner-friendly, and designed to help participants create a one-page project plan. Do not claim that it guarantees project success, professional certification, employment, income, or a particular health or well-being result.
