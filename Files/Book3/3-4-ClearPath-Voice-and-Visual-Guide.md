# ClearPath Voice and Visual Guide

> Fictional sample file for *ChatGPT Visual Bible*. All brand details are invented for teaching.

## Voice

Write in plain, friendly language for adults who are new to project planning.

- Address the reader as **you**.
- Use short paragraphs and familiar words.
- Explain any project-management term when it first appears.
- State practical details such as date, time, location, price, and accessibility.
- Describe benefits in specific terms.
- End promotional copy with one clear next step.

## Avoid

- Hype, pressure, and fear of missing out
- Promises or guarantees
- Claims that a workshop is effortless or will change someone's life
- Unexplained abbreviations
- Hashtags and emoji unless the user asks for them
- Invented quotations or participant stories

## Visual identity

Use these colors when creating a draft promotional image:

- Deep teal: `#165B5C`
- Warm yellow: `#F2C14E`
- Soft cream: `#FFF8E8`
- Charcoal: `#263238`

Do not rely on color alone to communicate information. Use clear labels, readable text, and strong contrast.

## Image direction

- Show adults working together around a table or joining a live online session.
- Include notebooks, sticky notes, or a simple project plan when useful.
- Use a warm editorial illustration style rather than glossy corporate photography.
- Leave open space for a title and event details.
- Avoid tiny text, busy backgrounds, handshakes, upward arrows, light bulbs, robots, and floating interface screens.
- Represent participants with a natural mix of ages, skin tones, and visible or non-visible working styles. Do not use stereotypes.

## Required review

Treat every image and piece of promotional copy as a draft. A person must check names, dates, prices, accessibility claims, contact details, and image details before publication.
