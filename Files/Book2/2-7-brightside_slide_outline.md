# Slide Outline — Brightside Learning Q2 Scheduling Recommendation
7 slides, 7-minute presentation + Q&A

---

### Slide 1 — Title: "Next Quarter's Workshop Schedule: A Recommendation"

**One point to remember:** We're here to decide which courses and formats to schedule more, hold steady, or scale back next quarter.

**Evidence on slide:** None (title slide) — presenter, date, and the decision framed as a question: "Which courses and delivery formats should we schedule more often next quarter?"

**Visual:** Simple title layout; no chart. Optional: Brightside Learning name/logo placeholder.

**Speaker notes purpose:** State up front that a decision is being requested today, not just a data review, and name the two people whose approval is needed.

---

### Slide 2 — Business Question & Measures

**One point to remember:** The recommendation rests on three measures — net revenue per session, attendance rate, and satisfaction — calculated from 28 cleaned sessions run April–June 2026.

**Evidence on slide:** Definitions from the Read Me sheet: net revenue = gross revenue − refunds; attendance rate = (bookings − no-shows) ÷ bookings; satisfaction = average of stated 1–5 scores. Note the dataset: 4 courses × 2 formats, 7 sessions per course, 14 sessions per format.

**Visual:** A simple 3-box diagram or small table listing the three measures and their one-line definitions (pulled directly from the Read Me sheet).

**Speaker notes purpose:** Explain why these three measures (not just revenue) were chosen, and briefly note the data was cleaned before analysis (one duplicate row removed, text inconsistencies standardized) — sets up credibility without dwelling on it.

---

### Slide 3 — Performance by Course

**One point to remember:** Project Planning and AI at Work lead on net revenue per session; Excel Essentials trails on revenue, attendance, and satisfaction alike.

**Evidence on slide:** From the Analysis sheet, Summary by course: avg net revenue/session (known) — Project Planning $1,967.50, AI at Work $1,827.14, Present with Confidence $1,538.57, Excel Essentials $1,382.14. Attendance rate — AI at Work 93.9%, Present with Confidence 89.9%, Project Planning 88.8%, Excel Essentials 86.4%. Satisfaction — AI at Work 4.69, Present with Confidence 4.59, Project Planning 4.4, Excel Essentials 4.2.

**Visual:** Bar chart of average net revenue per session by course, sorted highest to lowest (this exact chart data is already provided in the Analysis sheet). Optionally a small table alongside with attendance rate and satisfaction per course for reference.

**Speaker notes purpose:** Walk through the revenue ranking, then note that AI at Work also leads attendance and satisfaction — reinforcing it as a strong, well-rounded performer — while Excel Essentials is the consistent laggard across all three measures.

---

### Slide 4 — Performance by Delivery Format

**One point to remember:** In-person sessions convert bookings into attendees more reliably than online, even though online draws more total bookings.

**Evidence on slide:** From the Analysis sheet, Summary by delivery format: attendance rate — In-person 93.1%, Online 87.2%. Total bookings — Online 297, In-person 233. Avg net revenue/session (known) — Online $1,718.93, In-person $1,613.46. Avg satisfaction — In-person 4.58, Online 4.35.

**Visual:** Side-by-side (grouped) bar chart or simple two-column table comparing Online vs. In-person on attendance rate, avg net revenue per session, and satisfaction.

**Speaker notes purpose:** Frame the in-person attendance advantage as a reliability point (fewer no-shows), not a revenue argument, since the revenue gap between formats is narrow — set up the "lean in-person" recommendation on the next slide without overstating it.

---

### Slide 5 — Recommended Scheduling Priorities

**One point to remember:** Increase Project Planning and AI at Work, maintain Present with Confidence, reconsider Excel Essentials, and lean toward in-person where feasible.

**Evidence on slide:** Direct synthesis of Slides 3–4: the two courses leading on net revenue per session (Project Planning, AI at Work) vs. the course trailing on all three measures (Excel Essentials); the format with the higher attendance rate and satisfaction (In-person).

**Visual:** A simple 4-row status table — Course/Format | Recommendation (Increase / Maintain / Reconsider / Lean toward) — e.g., a traffic-light or icon-coded list rather than a chart.

**Speaker notes purpose:** Explicitly state this is a direction for approval, not a specific session count — no future session numbers have been set. Reiterate that satisfaction was close across the board (4.2–4.7) and did not independently drive the call.

---

### Slide 6 — Data Limitations & Risks

**One point to remember:** Two unresolved data issues limit confidence in the exact figures — the direction of the finding still holds, but both should be confirmed before the schedule is finalized.

**Evidence on slide:** From the Data Issues and Cleaned Sessions sheets: session S-107 (Project Planning) is missing its refund amount and was excluded from every net revenue calculation, so Project Planning's leading revenue figure is based on 6 of 7 sessions. Session S-119 (Project Planning) shows 23 bookings against a listed capacity of 20, unexplained and uncorrected. Also note: session S-111 is missing its satisfaction score (excluded from satisfaction averages); the analysis covers one quarter only.

**Visual:** Small table listing the two open issues with session ID, what's missing/wrong, and what it affects (kept plain — no chart needed).

**Speaker notes purpose:** Be direct that these two items sit under the strongest number in the deck (Project Planning's revenue lead), and name the concrete next step — confirm both with the data owner — rather than letting the caveat undercut the recommendation.

---

### Slide 7 — Decision & Next Steps

**One point to remember:** Approve the direction — increase Project Planning and AI at Work, hold Present with Confidence, reconsider Excel Essentials, lean in-person — pending confirmation of the two flagged data issues.

**Evidence on slide:** Restate the four-course/format calls from Slide 5 as the specific approval being requested; restate the two confirmation items from Slide 6 as pending actions (no dates, budgets, or session counts, since none are in the workbook).

**Visual:** Simple decision checklist (what's being approved today vs. what's still open) — text-based, no chart.

**Speaker notes purpose:** Close by asking explicitly for sign-off and inviting questions; keep this slide as the anchor to return to if Q&A wanders into supporting detail.
